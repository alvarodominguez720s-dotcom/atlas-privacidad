What Atlas does

🏋️

A gym routine built for you

Based on the days you train and your equipment: a gym, dumbbells at home or just your body. It's fixed with no end date: every day you know what to do. Edit all of it whenever you like. Over 160 exercises with their technique explained.

📸

Calories from a photo

Take a photo of your plate and Atlas estimates the calories and macros. Or describe it in your own words, scan the barcode or log it manually. Left half your plate? Photograph the leftovers and they're subtracted automatically.

💬

A trainer that knows your data

Ask "am I improving on bench press?" or "what do I still need to eat today?". It sees your workouts, meals and weight, and answers with your numbers, not generic advice.

📈

Clear progress

Personal records, a calendar history, a chart for every exercise and your real weight trend, without the scares of a day's water weight.

💧

Hydration tracking

Optional: how much water you should drink based on your weight, height, age and training days, and a bottle that fills up as you drink.

🤝

Train with your friends

Add them with a code and compare workouts, streaks and who lifts more. They only see how you train: never your weight or what you eat.

Frequently asked questions

Is Atlas free?

Yes. For free you get your fixed, editable routine, every exercise with its technique, your records and history, unlimited manual and barcode meal logging, weight tracking, hydration tracking, friends, 3 messages a day with the trainer and 1 AI meal analysis a day. No ads. Atlas Pro is optional: €4.99 a month or €39.99 a year, with a 7-day free trial.

Do I need a gym?

No. Atlas builds your routine around the equipment you have: a full gym, a pair of dumbbells at home or just your own body.

How does it count calories from a photo?

You take a photo of your plate and the AI identifies the food and estimates its calories, protein, carbs and fat. The photo is analysed and discarded: it is not stored. You can also describe what you ate or scan the barcode.

What is the fixed routine?

A routine that repeats every week with no end date. You pick the days you train and every day Atlas tells you which session is next. If you skip a day, next time you get the pending one. You can edit all of it and go back to the original whenever you like.

Is it available on iPhone?

Not yet: Atlas is on Android, on Google Play, and for now in Spain.

What happens with my data?

There are no ads and your data is not sold. You can download a copy of everything or delete your account from within the app whenever you want. It's all in the privacy policy.
